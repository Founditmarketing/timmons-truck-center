2023 IC Bus School CE Series PSI 8.8L Propane

  • You need school buses that reach their destinations with ironclad predictability. The CE Series is a type C school bus engineered from the tires up to withstand the rigors of daily use and get your passengers there on time, every time. Power your school bus with diesel, propane, or gasoline engines and access tools and resources to operate as efficiently as possible—all with a line of buses that are fun to drive.
  • Available in a number of different configurations, up to a 83-passenger capacity, the flexibility of the CE Series school bus can serve school districts of any size or geographical area with a reliable and surprisingly fun type C school bus.

Features may include:

  • Leave No Student Behind® rear safety feature: standard
  • 16-gauge steel body and 100% steel rub rail for added durability: standard
  • Evenly spaced entry steps—the widest in the industry
  • LCD on-board diagnostics with fault code reporting for easy maintenance
  • Three-piece flat, roped-in windshield and stationary glass for easy replacement
  • Electric passenger door with standard electric vandal lock on entrance door
  • Interchangeable BTI seating with precision, die-formed seat base and seat back
  • Wide-opening, easy-tilt three-piece fiberglass hood for improved access
